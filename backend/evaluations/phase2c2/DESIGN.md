# Prespecified nonlearning scenario discovery

Base `b2007c3`. Final protocol version: **v2, nonoverlapping registration streams**. No DQN weights, reward, observation or action space changes. This phase makes no new learning model and loads no checkpoint. The fixed 18-PRB partition, source traffic files, 2/10-ms budgets, channels, jammer and local scheduling algorithms are preserved.

The final protocol first probes 24 candidate indices in each disjoint block (training 12001–12024, validation 13001–13024, future-model holdout 14001–14024). Actual placement seed is **4096 × index**. The unchanged legacy initializer derives each UE seed as scenario_seed+100000+slice_index×1000+UE_index (episode index zero). Adjacent scenario seeds reuse 14/16 registration seeds, and blocks 1000 apart reuse cross-slice streams. The stride prevents any such collision; validate all 72×16 derived seeds before simulation and reject 32-bit wraparound. Only offered volume, current rate/position/CQI/budget and quota diagnostics are retained from 20-ms initialization probes. Probe performance is not a selection input. Finite seed screening does not prove no other original-trace seed could meet a regime.

Let dt=1 ms; b_i is attainable bytes/(PRB tick), L is the constant offered batch bytes/UE/tick, D_i is the fixed UE queueing budget. Compute fluid demand A=sum_i L/b_i and integer one-tick demand C=sum_i ceil(L/b_i). Positive, static rates and source periodicity are verified. C<=Q is a sufficient **resource-layer** from-empty one-tick service construction at fixed quota Q; it is not a guarantee for every scheduler, existing backlog, 10-ms causal RIC response, eMBB SLA or industrial end-to-end deadline. A<Q alone is not a deadline certificate.

For a T-tick interval containing arrivals at dt,2dt,...,Tdt, count only arrivals strictly due before its end: n_i=max(0,ceil(T-D_i/dt-epsilon)-1), epsilon=1e-9 ticks. Exact-equality deadlines are conservatively excluded. Required work is W(T)=sum_i n_i*L/b_i. If W(T)>Q*T, zero deadline misses are impossible even with optimistic fractional PRBs, instantaneous allocation and no backlog at the start. This is a necessary-bound violation, not an optimal failure percentage or attribution to a particular UE. Ignoring inherited backlog and rounding makes the bound conservative. Apply it to a 1249-tick initial ON window and a 1250-tick OFF window. The current original jammer is unchanged (period 2500, duty .5); observed OFF occurs at tick1250.

Regimes (defined at severe jamming, accompanied by j0/j10 controls):

- **Feasible**: C<=54. All fresh one-ms batches can fit even within the smallest baseline quota without reserve. This is a constructive capacity condition with a zero-failure reference gate.
- **Contested**: W(1249)>54*1249, C<=72 and A<60. No-reserve ON service necessarily misses some deadlines; allocating sufficient reserve can physically fit each fresh batch in one tick, with OFF fluid headroom. This does not promise all-UE recovery under each scheduler. B0/B1/B2 must exhibit at least one percentage-point aggregate/worst-UE separation or differing recovery; otherwise report a failed coverage gate, not a replacement scenario.
- **Overloaded**: W(1250)>78*1250. Even unjammed/OFF service with all 18 PRBs optimistically assigned to URLLC cannot meet all deadlines. This distinguishes intrinsic aggregate infeasibility from bad reserve distribution or intra-slice service deprivation. Zero misses are not a sensible target there.

First select ascending unused original seeds meeting each criterion, two per regime per pool. If a bin lacks coverage, take the first remaining seed and reuse the Phase 1.2 length-column scaler on a separate generated CSV, with targets A=32.4/56.7/85.8 for feasible/contested/overloaded. Recompute certificates from the actual floored bytes. Original files are hash-checked before/after. Each generated copy preserves timestamps and all non-length fields. Original and scaled configurations are labeled; results are never silently pooled as the same workload.

Freeze the complete manifests **before** full B0/B1/B2 characterization. Do not change membership after reference results. Three pools each have six placement seeds (two/regime), with three severities per seed. Characterize exactly 162 reference episodes of three seconds, sequentially under the guard. No search-budget expansion if original coverage or reference gates fail. Baseline outputs are permitted design evidence, not blind policy-test results. Future holdout is **reference-characterized but model-unseen**, not untouched test data; a future joint-model evaluation must keep its identities fixed and avoid tuning from that evaluation.

Reference characterization reuses Phase 2C-1 B0/B1/B2. It checks complete paired exogenous and deadline hashes, integer reserve constraints, per-UE static rate min/max, source arrival sizes, baseline/capacity settings, and deadline partitions. Sparse after-service frames cover first failure, first OFF, the next ON and horizon. Resource observer data are never controller inputs. Summaries keep batch failures, right-censored not-yet-due traffic, administrative terminal charges, served-byte lateness and sampled HOL distinct.

The proposed future checkpoint key is regime/severity-balanced lexicographic: known failed/offered, mean scenario worst-UE failure, offered-failure upper bound, eMBB served/offered deficit, activated-reserve PRB-ticks/(18*service ticks), switches/completed control decisions, then earlier checkpoint. Count-based terms use exact rational fractions; eMBB deficit is rounded to 12 decimals (well below one byte's resolution), reserve to integer PRB-ticks, avoiding meaningless float ties. This is a prospective selection rule, not a reward change or reranking of current DQN models. It prioritizes aggregate misses and only resolves their ties with fairness, so it cannot guarantee per-UE nondegradation/Pareto improvement. Finite-horizon upper bounds can favor draining recent arrivals. Resource/switch tie-breaks must not be described as observed reliability gains. Report validation by regime and by independent placement seed, not one pooled seed or millisecond-level pseudo-replication.

Future ablations are recommendations only: same 30-input validity-aware state, unchanged joint reward/loss/optimizer/budget, reserve-only with fixed P/F, switch-only with fixed declared reserve rule, and full joint actions. Their implementations and training are outside this phase. Original 15-state/original-objective baselines remain complementary references rather than controls isolating joint decision value.

## Retired preliminary discovery

v1 used adjacent base seeds and was retired upon detecting the source-level stream overlap, including a direct check that Phase 2C-1 seeds3101/3102 share seven of eight URLLC positions. Its in-progress reference job was interrupted; the old protocol and retirement audit are preserved. This was a design-correctness revision, not membership/threshold tuning from controller performance. Operating definitions, scale targets, counts and zero-learning scope are unchanged. Final v2 starts fresh, freezes before reference characterization, and does not reuse v1 performance as evaluation evidence. Extra diagnostic work and the interrupted run's unavailable final memory peak are disclosed.
